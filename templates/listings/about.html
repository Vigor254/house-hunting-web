Why we built Blueprint Housing

Blueprint Housing was started by a student at Technical University of Mombasa who knows, first-hand, how stressful it is to find a decent, safe room near campus — scrolling through scattered WhatsApp groups, chasing agents who disappear, and sometimes paying for a room that doesn't look anything like the photos.

We built this platform to fix that: a single place where TUM students can see real rooms, bedsitters, hostels and guest houses near campus, sorted by distance and price, with real descriptions and photos — and a real person (not a bot) verifying every listing before it goes live.

How it works

  • Browse for free. View the map, filter by budget and room type, no sign-up needed just to look.
  • We verify for you. Every inquiry goes through Blueprint Housing directly — we confirm details with the landlord before you commit to anything.
  • Pay only once you commit. For individual rooms and bedsitters, a finder's fee (about a third of one month's rent) applies only after you decide to take the place — never before you've viewed it.
  • Hostels and guest houses work differently. Owners pay a small monthly subscription to be listed, so students browse those for free.

Where we're starting

We're starting right here in Mombasa, close to Technical University of Mombasa, so we can personally vet listings and build real trust with students and landlords alike. Once that foundation is solid, we plan to expand to Nairobi and other campuses.
